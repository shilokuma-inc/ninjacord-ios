No account or sign-in is required. To test sending, tap "Choose a destination" on the Sending tab, paste a Discord webhook URL under "Use an Unsaved URL", tap "Use This URL", and then tap "Send a message!".

New in this version: (1) Shortcuts: in the Shortcuts app, add the "Send to Discord" action from Ninja Cord, choose a saved destination (save one in the app first via "Choose a destination" > "Save Destination") and a template or a message, then run it. The message is sent in the background without opening the app, and appears in the Posted tab when send history is on. On the free plan, only the first template in the list can be used from Shortcuts; Ninja Cord Pro can use any template. (2) Forum and media channels: turn on "Forum or Media Channel" when saving a destination, or swipe left on a saved destination and tap "Set as Forum". A "Post title" field then appears in the Message section, and the message is sent as a new post with that title. (3) Send options: below "Attachment" on the Sending tab, open "Send options" to send silently, hide link previews, or not notify @everyone or @here.

Messages you send can be edited or deleted afterwards. Right after a successful send, tap "Undo" in the confirmation banner to delete the message. To see the list of sent messages, turn on "Save send history" (on the Posted tab or the Setting tab), send a message, and open the Posted tab: swipe a message to delete it, or tap it to edit its text and embed. Deleting is free; editing is part of Ninja Cord Pro, and can also be tried once by watching a rewarded ad.

The App Tracking Transparency prompt appears on first launch, right after the getting-started guide is closed (after Google's consent form in regions where it is required).
